How to read it: each line is one skill, 1 on the left and 99 on the right. The bright tick is where you are. An upgrade sits at the level it asks for, so anything left of your tick only needs its quest, and the first one to the right is your next unlock. Green means you qualify now, gold means you are within three levels. Skills are sorted by how close the next unlock is.
