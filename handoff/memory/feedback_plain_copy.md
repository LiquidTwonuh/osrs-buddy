When writing user-facing copy in the user's apps (patch notes, UI labels, blurbs, empty states — see [[project-osrs-todo]]), stick to plain descriptions of what things do. No quippy asides, forced jokes, or cutesy alliterative titles.

**Why:** User called out examples as "cringe": "builds, reminders, grudges" (a jokey list tail) and the patch title "Elements & elbow room". Their words: "just stick to what things do and good descriptions."

**How to apply:** Describe function directly ("Notes: a sticky-note board. Notes save as you type."). Descriptive titles ("Themes, notes, and renames" not "Elements & elbow room"). Trim filler like "obviously", "sorry, Sailing", "instead of silently judging you". Warmth is fine when the USER writes it (e.g. their love-note sign-off in Krystal Klear v1.0 — that stays verbatim); manufactured quirk from me is not. This likely extends to conversational tone preferences too — keep banter natural, not performative.

**No em-dashes (2026-09-10).** User: "quit using EM-dashes". Applies to app copy AND chat replies. Use a comma, a full stop, a colon, or parentheses instead. Rewriting the sentence is usually better than swapping in a substitute dash. (394 of them still exist in older OSRS Buddy copy; only the Scroll and newly-written text has been cleaned so far, a full sweep was offered and not yet requested.)
