On 2026-10-18 he decided the app is renamed **Mortimeter** (mor-tih-mee-ter): Mortimer, his favourite Slayer master, plus "meter" because the app measures your account. "OSRS Buddy" had to go because RSBuddy/OSBuddy was a well-known third-party client that ended badly with Jagex, and the names were one letter apart.

Runners-up he liked: Elder ("Elder App" rolls off the tongue), then Sage. He found Quill/Almanac/Scry/Augur/Morrow too tryhard or ambiguous. He rejected Mortimetrics (reads as a RuneMetrics knockoff, narrows the promise to stats).

**Rules that came out of it:**
- Keep "OSRS"/"RS" and other people's names out of the product name; the tagline carries the game: "for Old School RuneScape". Not "OSRS Companion": an Android app with exactly that name exists (com.dennyy.osrscompanion).
- Mortimer art must be our own (skull in a crested teal helm, purple cape, a meter motif). Never Jagex's model or renders. There is a RuneLite plugin "Mortimer Slayer" and an unrelated "Mortimer" time-tracking program; neither clashes.
- Bud (Ask Bud / Wise Old Bud) keeps his name for now. "Wise Old Claude" (bring-your-own Anthropic key, told as Bud ascending) is queued on the Board under Next.
- Krystal Klear stays Krystal Klear.

**How to apply:** the rename checklist (page copy, Tauri product names and identifiers com.joshf.mortimeter / .beta, save-file migration from com.joshf.osrsbuddy, Documents\Mortimeter backups, Android package, GitHub repo rename by him, icon brief) is on `BOARD.md` in the repo. Until the rename lands, files and folders are still named OSRS-Buddy; see [[project-osrs-backlog]] and [[feedback-release-cadence]].
