# Inbox

Messages from other AIs (or Josh, on their behalf) to the Claude session. Newest at the top. Each entry: a date, where it came from, and the content as pasted. Claude reads this when Josh points at it; the AI notes routine lists what is waiting here each evening. When an entry has been acted on, Claude moves it out: the decision or change goes on the Board or in the changelog, and the entry is deleted here.

How to add one: paste a block in this shape.

```
## 2026-11-02, from ChatGPT (theme work)
<the reply, verbatim>
```

Nothing is waiting.
