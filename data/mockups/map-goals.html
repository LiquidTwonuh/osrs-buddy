Map goal tiles

The popular goals under the Map search, three ways that are not pills. Quests have no icons of their own, so each direction leans on something we do have: the rewards, a colour per quest, your progress through its chain. All pictures are the wiki's skill icons, already in the app.

⌕

A · Reward cards

Lead with what you get. The biggest XP reward and its skill icon sit on top, the quest name under it, then how far through the chain you already are. The cape card is the whole log.

Why it might win: the rewards are the pitch, and the card says "you are already 17 of 34 of the way there". Costs the most height.

B · Banners

A colour per quest, a faint sigil in the corner, the name in the display face, and a progress ring. Identity comes from colour and type, the way the game's quest journal groups things by series.

Why it might win: the most "artsy" at a glance and still one row of information. The colours need choosing once per quest; the rest of the log would get a neutral banner.

C · Medallions

Seals in a row. The ring is your progress through the chain, the glyph is the top reward skill, the little number is the chain length. Compact enough to keep more than eight.

Why it might win: smallest and the most distinct from everything else in the app. Names get tight on long titles.
